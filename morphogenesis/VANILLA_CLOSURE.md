# Vanilla Morphogenesis Programme — Closure and Final Synthesis

This document closes the vanilla morphogenesis programme (stages M0, M0b, M0c, M1 and M2a), as of the closure instruction of 2026-10-08. No new simulations were run for it; it only collects what the earlier deliverables established. Every claim cites the file that established it; paths are relative to `morphogenesis/` unless they start with `stage6_flock/`. Labels are the programme's own: `ESTABLISHED` (measured and the controls passed), `PROVISIONAL` (measured, but limited by sample, base state or method), `NOT DONE`. Findings are kept on separate axes (shape, roles, dynamics, protocol); there is no single score.

`M2A` below means `m2a_audit_and_library/`.

## a) What the vanilla model is

The vanilla model is the published active-inference morphogenesis simulation (`DEM_morphogenesis.m` run by SPM12's `spm_ADEM`, driven from Python under Octave 10.3.0; `m0b_reference_port/PORT_DESIGN.md`, `M2A/AUDIT_M1.md` §0.2). In the standard configuration 8 cells (16 in the larger variant) live in a plane. Each cell has a position, secretes 4 signals, senses the signals around it, and holds beliefs over which of 8 cell types it is (64 beliefs in all); it moves and secretes so as to make what it senses match what its beliefs predict. Development follows a ramp that switches the sensory coupling on over the first few hundred bins (`M2A/PROTOCOL_V2.md`). Everything is deterministic: the process noise is identically zero (`M2A/AUDIT_M1.md` §0.2). The engine used for every number below is unmodified SPM12 `spm_ADEM`, plus one state-exporting copy of it that is bit-identical to SPM12 when nothing is imported (`M2A/CONTINUATION.md`, `M2A/tests/test_engine_equivalence.py`). The Python reimplementations never reached equivalence and were not used (`m0_reconstruction/README.md` banner; `m0c_port_completion/README.md` warning 2).

## b) ESTABLISHED findings

1. **One dominant shape plus a rare defective second attractor.** Under the published developmental clock, 97 of 100 individuals end in one shape (class 0) and 3 of 50 secondary draws end in a second (class 1); none of 50 primary draws do (`M2A/CENSUS_V2.md`). Class 1 has a duplicated role, a vacant role and an undifferentiated cell, and is mirror-asymmetric (`M2A/REANALYSIS.md` (e)). It is a real alternative attractor: it is also reached by large position kicks (6/200) and random displacement fields (8/60) (`M2A/ROBUSTNESS_V2.md`, `M2A/REANALYSIS.md` (b)). M1's slow clock hid it (`M2A/AUDIT_M1_ADDENDUM.md` item 3, 7). The 8-cell model has no other attractor that we found.
2. **The reference phenotype is a near-miss of the model's own target, caused by soft beliefs.** The census end state stays about 0.277 from the template, not at it; each cell sits near the belief-weighted average of template positions because beliefs are not concentrated (maximum belief 0.72–0.78). Beliefs explain most of the gap; a residual of 0.05–0.10 is not explained (`M2A/AUDIT_M1.md` §0.5, `M2A/CENSUS_V2.md`).
3. **Deterministic engine; individuals merge up to relabelling.** Different noise seeds give bit-identical runs (`M2A/AUDIT_M1.md` §0.2). Individuals differ only in their initial beliefs, and their trajectories converge to the same body up to a permutation of cells with a time constant of about 6.5 bins; role maps are effectively random permutations (98 distinct among 100) (`M2A/CENSUS_V2.md`). Consequence for every later statistic: the 8-cell mature body is one experiment, so the independent sample is the number of distinct bodies, not the number of individuals (`M2A/WITHDRAWAL_V2.md` n_eff columns).
4. **Strong contraction; withdrawal restores shape.** Both adult shapes are linearly stable fixed points, class 0 with a relaxation time of 7.9 bins and class 1 15.2 bins (`M2A/SKELETON.md` D1). After the double-head (DH), double-tail (DT) and single-anomalous-cell (AN) perturbations are withdrawn, the shape returns in all 300 runs, with no persistent or non-convergent outcome (`M2A/WITHDRAWAL_V2.md`); a full belief reset returns in 100/100 (`M2A/ROBUSTNESS_V2.md`). The qualification is class 1: large kicks and random fields can land there (item 1). All 480 positive small pulses of the revised library leave no trace (`M2A/LIBRARY.md`).
5. **Role reassignment is the only durable, non-defective change.** Perturbations that leave the shape unchanged still relabel which cell holds which role (DT on a mature body 20/20, though a random sham field does it as often; the single anomalous cell 6/20, a deterministic function of the targeted role; `M2A/WITHDRAWAL_V2.md`, `M2A/REANALYSIS.md` (a)). Spontaneous role switching was absent in 72,000 noisy bins (`M2A/NOISE.md`, `M2A/REANALYSIS.md` (c)), so the relabelling is intervention-induced.
6. **Minimal interventions** (`M2A/MINIMAL_PERTURBATIONS.md`; class-0 body, 246 directions, bisection; one body up to relabelling). A role swap with unchanged shape ("FATE-SWAP") needs a single-cell displacement of 0.68–8.1 position units (median 4.4) or a secretion pulse of 3.3–37.8; whole-body, regional and global pulses lie in the same range. A shape switch occurs in only 6 directions (a tail-end single-cell displacement, one whole-body eigen-direction, the global ligand-4 pulse), always into class 1 and always after a role swap at a lower amplitude. The tail-end role and region are the easiest to swap and the only role with any shape switch; mirror-image roles and regions agree exactly. Three thresholds replicate on two relabelled individuals (`M2A/SKELETON.md` D6). The class boundary, along the one direction examined, is a saddle with a single unstable eigenvalue (edge state approximate; `M2A/SKELETON.md` D5, `PROVISIONAL`).
7. **Hysteresis in the DH and DT loops.** Raising and then lowering the DH or DT strength quasi-statically returns a class-0 body to the same shape with permuted roles (DH a 5-cycle, DT a swap); a class-1 body collapses onto the class-0 trajectory under DH, and under DT is repaired into class 0 (`M2A/HYSTERESIS.md`). Class 1 → class 0 is the only durable shape change found that moves toward a defect-free form. The down-sweep from class 1 under DH was not run (inferred).
8. **The slow modes are almost entirely hidden.** In the one-bin map's Jacobian (224 states) the eight slowest class-0 modes are 96–100 % carried by the belief block, and class 1's slowest is 88 % belief; the observable part (positions and secretions) relaxes quickly (`M2A/SKELETON.md` D1). The unstable mode at the edge state is 88 % belief (`M2A/SKELETON.md` D5).
9. **The 16-cell landscape is multi-class.** Thirty 16-cell individuals fall into 10 clusters (sizes 17, 2, 2, 2, 2, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1); the dominant shape holds 17/30 (57 %, Wilson 39–73 %), against about 97 % in the 8-cell body. The cluster count depends on the clustering distance, and no 16-cell template audit was done (`M2A/GENERALIZATION.md` §1.1, `PROVISIONAL`). Whether the 16-cell alternatives are defective was not examined.

## c) NEGATIVE results (stated as negatives)

- **No defect-free durable shape change from the healthy body.** Across 246 D4 directions (2,309 evaluations, no non-convergence), 300 withdrawal runs, 200 kicks and the hysteresis loops, no intervention moved the class-0 body to a different, defect-free shape; every durable shape change is a move into class 1 (`M2A/MINIMAL_PERTURBATIONS.md`, `M2A/WITHDRAWAL_V2.md`, `M2A/ROBUSTNESS_V2.md`, `M2A/HYSTERESIS.md`). This holds for the tested base state, directions and amplitude brackets (0.05–10 displacement, 0.05–50 pulse); "no threshold" means none up to the top of the bracket. It depends on classing class 1 as a defect, which is my classification from the role and belief audit (`M2A/REANALYSIS.md` (e); `M2A/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` item 2).
- **No hysteresis in the sensory-precision multiplier.** Up and down sweeps from both shapes recover the baseline (`M2A/HYSTERESIS.md`, `PREC` rows).
- **The published clump phenotype was not reproduced.** With the declared interpretation of the high-sensory-precision manipulation, no clump-like phenotype appeared at any of the three pilot values (distance to the reference only 0.07–0.13); the full runs gave class 0 in 9/10 individuals and one other shape (distance 0.336) (`M2A/GENERALIZATION.md` §1.2). The scope is narrow: three pilot values and one interpretation (the m0c-declared one); I do not claim the paper's result is wrong. The other manipulation (sensitivity ×2) did produce a robust novel shape, not described as a clump (same section).
- **No effect of seeded initial identity on final roles.** Seeding 2 or 4 cells with high initial identity expectation does not keep the seeded roles (`M2A/GENERALIZATION.md` §1.3, n = 10 each, `PROVISIONAL`).
- **M1's "AN" results and its noise floor are void** (see e).

## d) UNRESOLVED

1. **The period-2 cycle: model or scheme.** The one-bin map has an eigenvalue −1.54 at the sustained-DH state (a flip), the unstable mode lives mostly in the action and previous-action blocks, and the two-bin monodromy is stable (0.823). The cycle also appears in the DT family at other strengths, and coincides with role transitions. All of that fits a model mechanism and fits a discretisation artefact equally well; the decisive test needs a finer integrator, and the engine diverges under sub-stepping for any perturbation (`M2A/SKELETON.md` D2, `M2A/HYSTERESIS.md`).
2. **Non-monotone threshold windows.** 29 of 246 D4 directions have a label that is not monotone in amplitude. The mechanism was not investigated, and the 4-point grid used for single-cell displacement means the true count is higher (`M2A/MINIMAL_PERTURBATIONS.md`, `M2A/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` item 6).
3. **Role fuzziness.** Role labels are slots from a Hungarian match to the class-0 template. Class-0 per-cell residuals to the template run from 0.003 to 0.592 and belief maxima are only 0.72–0.78 (`M2A/CENSUS_V2.md`). Whether the permuted roles after the DH and DT loops, and the FATE-SWAP counts, are real changes of fate or an artefact of matching on near-degenerate slots was not checked independently (`M2A/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` item 10).
4. **Noise sensitivity of role outcomes.** The noise-free result that DH on a mature body returns the same roles (0/20) becomes a relabelling in 10/10 at 1 % noise, while spontaneous switching stays at zero; this is one noise realisation per individual and level (`M2A/NOISE.md`, `M2A/REANALYSIS.md` (c)). How the D4 thresholds move under noise is `NOT DONE` (`M2A/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` items 11, 13).
5. Also not done: D4 from the class-1 body and above the brackets; D5 from other directions; the original full perturbation-response library (dropped by instruction) (`M2A/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` items 7, 8, `M2A/README.md`).

## e) Protocol corrections made along the way (v1 → v2)

M1 data are "protocol v1"; everything from M2a on is "protocol v2". The corrections, all in `M2A/AUDIT_M1_ADDENDUM.md` unless noted:

- **M1's single-anomalous-cell runs never applied the anomaly** (an empty cell list was passed); they were bit-identical to DH. Corrected by a re-run under v1 settings and by `M2A/WITHDRAWAL_V2.md` (`M2A/AUDIT_M1.md` §0.1c). This was the one failed Part 0 gate.
- **M1's kicks and "mature body" conditions were re-developments**, restarted with the ramp at zero. v2 uses continuations of the full state, which showed new-form outcomes v1 could not produce and showed that DH on a mature body returns the same roles (v1's 20/20 relabelling was largely the restart) (item 2; `M2A/CONTINUATION.md`).
- **M1's clock depended on run length.** v2 uses one absolute clock with the published development time (32 bins); the slow clock had hidden the second shape class (item 3; `M2A/PROTOCOL_V2.md`).
- **M1's "noise floor" never operated** because process noise is zero; v2 treats zero noise as canonical and adds noise only as a flagged extension (item 4; `M2A/NOISE.md`).
- **M1's stationarity times were set by the ramp.** v2 uses a state-based criterion (item 5; `M2A/THRESHOLDS_v2.md`).
- **Role relabelling was invisible to M1's distance** and is now part of the outcome taxonomy (`M2A/AUDIT_M1.md` §0.1; `M2A/WITHDRAWAL_V2.md`).
- **Replication unit:** adult replicates are one experiment; every v2 table carries an effective-n column (`M2A/WITHDRAWAL_V2.md`).
- **Earlier:** the stage-M0 Python solver was an approximation and is deprecated; the Python port never matched the oracle step-for-step (`m0_reconstruction/README.md`, `m0c_port_completion/README.md`).

## g) Why the programme moves to a modified testbed

The vanilla model lacks the following properties; the first and fourth are measured, the second is a premise of this closure that no vanilla document tests, and the others are measured as noted.

- **No defect-free multistability.** The only alternative attractor of the 8-cell body is defective (duplicated and vacant role, undifferentiated cell) and is reached only by large impulses; the healthy body has no second, equally good shape to switch to (`M2A/CENSUS_V2.md`, `M2A/MINIMAL_PERTURBATIONS.md`). A programme about controlling shape needs a body with more than one acceptable form. The 16-cell variant has more clusters, but their quality was not audited (`M2A/GENERALIZATION.md`).
- **Static, and pinned to absolute coordinates.** The vanilla body relaxes to a stationary arrangement (every census run reaches a fixed point, `M2A/CENSUS_V2.md`) that is compared with a template in fixed coordinates; no vanilla stage tested translation, drift or motion, so that this is a property of the model is a premise taken from the closure instruction, `NOT DONE` as a test. It matters because the preceding flock programme worked with translating collectives (`stage6_flock/TRANSLATING_FLOCK_FINAL_SYNTHESIS.md`), which a body that settles to a fixed arrangement cannot represent.
- **No noise.** The engine is deterministic and the individual is defined by initial beliefs alone (`M2A/AUDIT_M1.md` §0.2); intrinsic variability, spontaneous switching and robustness cannot be studied, and the one noise extension showed role outcomes are not stable under noise (`M2A/NOISE.md`).
- **Hidden slow modes.** The slow relaxation is 96–100 % in the belief block (`M2A/SKELETON.md` D1), and the boundary between shapes is crossed along a belief-dominated direction (D5). A controller or observer that sees only positions and secretions cannot see the state that determines the outcome, which makes an observable-only analysis structurally incomplete.
- **Soft roles.** Beliefs are unconcentrated, residuals to template slots are large and roles are random permutations between individuals (`M2A/AUDIT_M1.md` §0.5, `M2A/CENSUS_V2.md`); a "role" is a matching convention, not a held identity, and role-change counts inherit that (`M2A/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` item 10).
- **An ill-posed discrete scheme.** The scheme does not converge as the step shrinks: any perturbation diverges under sub-stepping, and the period-2 cycle is a flip of the one-bin map whose origin cannot be tested (`M2A/SKELETON.md` D2). Dynamical statements about the vanilla model are therefore statements about this particular discretisation.

## h) What the next programme may and may not assume

**May assume (as `ESTABLISHED`):**
- The Octave `spm_ADEM` and the state-exporting copy reproduce the published scheme and each other bit-for-bit, and continuation from a saved state is exact (`M2A/AUDIT_M1.md` §0.2, `M2A/CONTINUATION.md`).
- Vanilla numbers in this document as regression references for any new testbed that reduces to the vanilla model: class 0 and class 1 shapes, the Jacobian spectra, the FATE-SWAP and SHAPE-SWITCH thresholds along the listed directions, the hysteresis loops (files cited above).
- The tools in (f), and the discipline they enforce: continuations rather than restarts, matched unperturbed twins, outcomes split into shape and role, Wilson intervals, effective sample size, and labelled claims.
- Pulses of amplitude 0.03 are linear to about 5 % on position and secretion channels, and not to 5 % on two field-gain channels (`M2A/LINEARITY.md`).

**May not assume:**
- That any protocol-v1 (M1) number holds; the AN rows, the noise floor, the stationarity times and the mature-body kick results are superseded (`M2A/AUDIT_M1_ADDENDUM.md`).
- That individuals are independent samples, that role labels are identities, or that a role swap is a change of fate in a sense stronger than the slot matching defines (b3, d3).
- That the vanilla thresholds, the privileged tail-end role, or the hysteresis loops transfer to a modified model; they are one body, one clock, zero noise, and one direction's worth of evidence for the saddle.
- That the period-2 cycle is physical (d1), that sub-stepping is available, or that the Python port is a substitute for the oracle.
- That the blind package may be used or released: it is archived NOT FOR RELEASE, and no blind analysis was ever run on it (`m2_blind_package/README.md`).
- That the viewer renders: it was built, not opened.
- Anything about the 16-cell variant beyond its census clusters, or about noise beyond the single-realisation extension.

Nothing in `stage6_flock/` or in the earlier stage deliverables (M0, M0b, M0c, M1) was modified by M2a or by this closure.
