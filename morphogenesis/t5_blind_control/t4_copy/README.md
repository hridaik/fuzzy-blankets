## Headline answers

**1. Can organisms be detected and tracked online at each observation level, and how well (internal consistency)?**
- O1 (tracked cells) - ESTABLISHED. Link-radius single-linkage detection with hysteresis plus forward largest-overlap identity. Zero false SPLIT/MERGE/EXTRUSION/ARRIVAL/LOSS flags on 260 natural dishes (200 dev + 60 held-out).
- O2 (unlabelled points) - ESTABLISHED. Own cell tracker (registration candidates + Hungarian on position + signal fingerprint + block-displacement vote). Against O1 ids: 100.0 % correct links on natural dishes (dev 200, held-out 60); 98.9 % (dev) / 99.4 % (held-out) in operation runs, 99.6-99.7 % in light/device runs. Event-level agreement with O1: SPLIT matched 52/58 dev, 6/8 held-out; MERGE 32/32 dev, 2/2 held-out. Failures are exactly the frames where the operation moves cells up to ~8 units between consecutive frames (positions and signals jump: unobservable continuity).
- O3 (images) - PROVISIONAL / partly NEGATIVE. The fluorescence fields are *smooth* (effective blob sd about 1.4 units vs cell spacing about 1), so **individual cells are not resolvable**: pseudo-cell positions match O1 cells at 1.1-1.4 units rms (about one cell spacing). What works at O3: organism detection by field foreground (count of organisms agrees with O1 in 100 % of natural frames, 82-83 % in operation runs), centroid (median error 0.7 units, a systematic bias of the intensity centroid), major axis (|cos| median 0.95), orientation sign from a field asymmetry (99 % agreement with O1 on natural dishes, 89-91 % in operation runs), cell count from integrated mass (error -1.0 +- 0.4 cells on natural dishes; large negative error when the body drifts to the window edge). What does not work: cell-level tracking (NOT DONE), cell-level events EXTRUSION (NOT DONE) and cell-ID-based material continuity (replaced by a field-overlap proxy, labelled as such), SPLIT/MERGE detection (matches only 3-6 of 58 dev O1 splits; fragments closer than the blur scale stay connected).

**2. Do material, structural and state layers dissociate, and where?** (ESTABLISHED for the qualitative pattern; paired with twins, held-out)
Yes. In operation runs with a twin, every treated founder leaves the strict validity envelope (V = 0 for all 24 dev + 15 held-out treated triplets at O1) while twins/shams mostly stay inside. Material (C1), structure (C2) and state (C3) separate: in light-treated runs (T3a/T3b/T4a/T4b; 96 treated runs, dev + held-out) **C1 persists in 100 %** (no cell leaves or arrives, no SPLIT/MERGE/EXTRUSION/ARRIVAL/LOSS flag at all) while **C2 changes in 69-100 %** and **C3 changes in 54-82 %** (breaks in 0-31 %). In cut/replacement/tweezer runs (P01-P03, P09) material turnover and break go together with structural and state change; in dish-merge runs (P04-P08) bodies are re-organised into fragments. Dissociation table: `EVENTS_AND_PAIRS.md`. Caveat: untreated twins/shams are not perfectly stationary relative to the natural calibration (slow drift of left-right pattern features, OOD state labels in 25-50 % of twins by the end of long runs), so the paired difference, not the absolute verdict, is the primary quantity.

**3. How many persistent collective states, and how confidently online?** (ESTABLISHED for a mirror-image pair; PROVISIONAL for the rest)
Natural bodies fall into **two equally common, persistent, mirror-image organisations (about 48 % each of dishes)**, distinguished by the *sign* of left/right (reflection-sensitive) channel asymmetries (e.g. c0 dipole -0.146 vs +0.145 across the body axis); a third, rare morphology (4 % of dishes: wider, looser, c4~c5) exists and itself comes as a mirror pair (O2 resolves it as two states of 4 dishes each). Reflection-invariant descriptors alone do **not** separate the two common states unless the c4/c5 channels are visible (O1, O3b). Number of states chosen by held-out likelihood + half-split stability: O1 k=3 (two common + one merged rare), O2 k=4, O3a k=2, O3b k=2 (half-split ARI 0.97-1.00). Online estimator (frozen GMM + sticky HMM filter + OOD label): validation dishes 98 % of frames carry the dish's modal label, mean posterior ~1.0, 0.09-0.16 false changes per dish, splice test (switching dish identities) detects 100 % with median latency 0 frames at the natural sampling (100 time units); with the 100-time-unit causal window used in fast runs the effective latency is up to about 50 time units. Details and the discoverability-vs-channels table: `STATES.md`.

**4. Which observables anticipate state changes, and by how much?** (NEGATIVE / not established)
No observable shows robust held-out anticipation. Nominal skill (hit rate minus time-shifted null) is at most about 0.05; at O1 1 of 46 features has a CI above zero on held-out (c4_mean, skill 0.045, lead about 14 time units) and it was not among the development top features; at O3b none. Leads of 45-130 time units seen in development have CIs including zero. Observational only, no mechanism. `ANTICIPATION.md`.

**5. Which partitions behave as blankets?** (PROVISIONAL; trivial-looking, reported plainly)
Channel-type partitions with the dish-level channels {c4,c5} kept apart from the type channels {c0-c3}, with the graded channel c6 or the type channels as B, have leakage far below random partitions of equal sizes (e.g. L = 0.028 vs null median 0.44 dev; 0.074 vs 0.55 held-out, null percentile 0.000) - the Louvain communities on predictive influence recover the same split ({c4,c5} vs the rest). This is because the channels are nearly independent across channels but strongly correlated across regions of the same channel; body-frame **region** partitions (head/mid/tail) are not blankets (null percentile 0.19-0.92). No directed asymmetry: no group-to-group lag-1 predictive gain has a CI above zero (held-out predictive gain is zero or negative). Sample size: n = 200 dishes vs dim 21 (n/dim 9.5, requirement n >= 5 dim met) in development; held-out one-frame-per-dish n = 60 (n/dim 2.9) **fails** the requirement (all-frames n_eff = 142, ratio 6.8, passes). `INFOTHEORY.md`.

What was verified by eye: cells/body outlines/axes line up with the O3 image blobs (orientation of the image panel matches the arena), V/V_cons badges and OOD grey state bars agree with the JSON, event ticks and the onset marker appear on the timeline at the right place, compare mode shows the event body fragmenting while twin and sham stay intact. Visible weaknesses: traces are clipped at 3x the envelope bound, the whole-run arena scale makes fragmented bodies small, the O3 outline (pseudo-cells) is smaller than the fluorescence blob, and in P01 runs the O3 organism list misses the fragment whose c6 level collapsed (field foreground is c6-based).
