## Headline answers
**1. What changes the collective state durably while preserving the body? (ESTABLISHED, dev sysid + held-out)**
Two light channels, each in one direction only: **L4 drives S+ -> S-, L3 drives S- -> S+** (the two persistent mirror-image organisations, read from the c0 left/right dipole sign and the c4/c5 dish-level means). A sufficient pulse at the body centre flips the state completely and permanently (in all 174 successful controller/fixed/whole-body episodes the state never left the target after first being sure in it; held at the episode end in 95 % of controlled held-out dishes; the failures were sub-threshold relapses *before* a flip) while **material and geometry are untouched** (0 SPLIT/MERGE/EXTRUSION/LOSS/ARRIVAL in 428 held-out and 700+ development episodes; V_body as good as the untreated twin) - "same body, other collective state". L1, L2, L5 only change pattern and/or damage the body (SPLIT/EXTRUSION) at the doses that do anything. No transient-only actuator was found. Sub-threshold pulses cause an excursion that decays (10-45 tu); the dose-response is threshold-like and the point of no return depends on history.

**2. Dose vs location and duration (ESTABLISHED within 4 dishes; PROVISIONAL as a population statement)** (SYSID.md)
Minimum effective duration ~5 tu (2 tu never flips at amp up to 80); from 10 tu on the needed integral amp x dur is constant (about 21-47 at the centre), and total dose (amp x dur x cells) is smallest at 20-40 tu (centre ~165-205). By location: centre 1x; side 1.3x; tail ~1.6x; **head 3-5x**; whole body ~1.5x (but needs 3.7x the controller's actual dose in the held-out because it is applied at the controller's amplitude). L4 (to S-) needs ~15 % less than L3. Location explains 54 % of the variance of log dose* (duration 20 %, dish 11 %, direction 3 %). The cue "response slowing" is real (recovery half-time 10 -> 44 tu as the excursion approaches threshold) but carries **no information beyond the excursion size** about the remaining gap (leave-one-dish-out R2 0.44 vs 0.37-0.42): NEGATIVE, not used.

**3. Does the online controller beat the random-location, fixed and whole-body baselines on held-out dishes? (mixed; NEGATIVE against fixed)** (EVALUATION.md, n = 60 dishes, 30 per direction)
* vs **random location at the same dose per pulse**: yes, decisively: success 0.93 vs 0.07 (paired +0.87, CI [0.77, 0.95], permutation p < 5e-5).
* vs **whole body** (same amplitude): similar success (0.93 vs 0.98, n.s.) at **3.7x lower dose**; a dose-matched whole body fails (0.02).
* vs **fixed single pulse tuned on development**: **no, it does not beat it**: success 0.93 vs 0.98 (diff -0.05, CI [-0.12, 0], p = 0.25), same dose (266 vs 259), slower (71 vs 38 tu). The controller's failures (3/60) were a commit threshold that was too eager plus a slow un-commit (post-freeze finding, not fixed); the fixed pulse benefits from being tuned on the same development dishes. The dish-to-dish threshold spread (x1.4) leaves little for adaptivity to gain; the benefits of the loop here are identity guarding (abort), and robustness rather than dose.

**4. Success rate with body identity preserved? (held-out, ESTABLISHED for this dish type)**
Controller **93.3 % [86.7, 98.3]** (S+ -> S-: 93.3 %, S- -> S+: 93.3 %), V_body and V_body_conservative 98.3 % - exactly equal to the untreated twin: the one violating dish is a natural cohesion false alarm of the frozen monitor that also occurs in the twin, sham and all arms. Controller minus CRN twin on state progress +1.48 [1.38, 1.56] (full change = 1.56); sham equals twin exactly. Dose to success 261 [240, 284], 2.4 pulses, 71 tu from first action to a sustained target state, SUCCESS requires 120 tu hold after the last action + last 40 tu sure target + V_body_conservative. 2 of 68 drawn dishes were excluded before any action (natural false alarm at baseline), 6 skipped for direction quota (reported).
