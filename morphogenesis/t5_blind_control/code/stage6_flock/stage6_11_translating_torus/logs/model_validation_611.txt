V3c. WHY. `policy_posterior` self-tunes its precision, W = alpha / (spm_beta -
     u.G), refitted from W = 0 every step. For |u.G| >> spm_beta that makes
     W ~ 1/|G|, so W*G -- the only thing the softmax sees -- is already
     approximately invariant to multiplying G by a constant. Scaling a
     synthetic G by c and reading back mean max u:
       c =  0.125   mean max u = 0.5894
       c =  0.250   mean max u = 0.6736
       c =  0.500   mean max u = 0.7261
       c =  1.000   mean max u = 0.7553
       c =  2.000   mean max u = 0.7707
       c =  4.000   mean max u = 0.7786
       c =  8.000   mean max u = 0.7826

  READING -- THE CLAIM DOES NOT HOLD, AND THE REASON IS NOT THE NORMALIZATION.
  A per-bird scalar is the wrong instrument here: the quantity that sharpens
  with degree is not the magnitude of G but the shape of the gap between the
  best action and the rest, and W*G is nearly blind to magnitude. Raw and
  normalized(k_ref=8) track each other to within a few thousandths of max u
  at every degree measured. Degree normalization is therefore retained as an
  available, explicitly labelled modelling variant, but it should NOT be
  described as a fix for the Stage 6.9 saturation, because it is not one.

  One thing a scalar DOES move is exact saturation, which is a property of the
  absolute size of W*G rather than of its shape: k_ref = 1 takes the fraction
  of birds at max u == 1.0 to zero while leaving mean max u ~ 0.95, i.e. the
  same decisions with a representable residual instead of an underflowed one.
  Whether that matters for interface recovery is a question for a later stage;
  this stage runs no inference and makes no claim about it.

  READING -- THE AUDIT'S HYPOTHESIS IS NOT SUPPORTED BY THIS SWEEP.
  For fc_pos <= 2, the largest change in Rg at fixed R is 0.50. Each cell
  mean is over 5 seeds whose across-seed sd runs 0.09-1.32 (median 0.54),
  i.e. a standard error on the cell mean of up to 0.59. Every cohesion effect on Rg
  is inside that. The group's fate is set by R alone: it disperses to Rg ~ 6
  for R <= 0.5 with or without cohesion, and binds for R >= 0.9 with or without
  it. fc_pos = 4 is actively harmful -- it overrides the alignment term, the
  group loses its common heading and comes apart, and realized degree
  collapses. The one cell that formally satisfies the hypothesis (R=1.2,
  fc_pos=1.0) improves Rg by 0.11 and degree by 0.73 against that standard error;
  it is not evidence.

  So the term is honest about what it is: a genuinely missing generic
  ingredient, added once and recorded, which does NOT rescue the Stage 6.9
  range-versus-degree trade-off. Section M's audit proposed that trade-off as
  a hypothesis and it is reported here as refuted for this statistic. The term
  stays OFF by default and no parameter of it has been chosen on any outcome.

  ONE UNANTICIPATED EFFECT, reported because it is large and reproducible
  across the sweep rather than because it was looked for. At matched R AND
  matched Rg -- i.e. the same range holding the group equally tightly --
  turning cohesion on collapses the exact-saturation fraction:

  At R = 0.9 the two cells have Rg 3.85 vs 3.86 and degree 21.7 vs 25.8 -- the
  cohesion-on cell is if anything MORE connected -- yet saturation falls from
  0.84 to 0.09. Section N tried to reach that by rescaling G and could not
  (V3); the cohesion term reaches it by changing G's SHAPE, adding a
  per-partner contribution whose direction is the bearing rather than the
  consensus heading, which narrows the gap between the best action and the
  rest. This is a measurement on uncontrolled runs, not a mechanism claim and
  not a control or inference result; nothing downstream has been run that it
  could have been tuned for. Whether a non-saturated policy actually restores
  a recoverable causal interface is exactly the question Stage 6.9 left open,
  and it belongs to a later section of this stage, not to this file.

==========================================================================
V5. NO TRAJECTORY ENGINEERING
==========================================================================
Three exact symmetries are checked directly on G. Together they say the new
terms cannot express a preferred direction, a preferred bird, or a preferred
place -- there is no argument slot in either of them for one.

  variant                      rot 90 dev   relabel dev   translate dev
  raw / no cohesion              1.42e-14      7.11e-15        0.00e+00
  normalized                     2.84e-14      1.42e-14        0.00e+00
  cohesion=1                     1.42e-14      7.11e-15        2.13e-14
  normalized + cohesion=1        4.26e-14      1.42e-14        1.36e-13

  All deviations are at floating-point accumulation noise (~1e-14), so:
   * rotating the world 90 degrees only permutes G's columns -- no term
     prefers north, east, or the direction of any Stage 6.9/6.10 result;
   * relabelling the birds only permutes G's rows -- no term reads an index,
     a group label, or a membership set;
   * translating the world leaves G unchanged -- no term reads an absolute
     position or a target location.

  By construction as well as by measurement: `_accumulate_G` sees only the
  live edge list, the relative displacements and the partners' headings. It
  is given no time index, so neither term can act as a schedule or a cue, and
  the cohesion direction is the mean unit bearing to whoever happens to be in
  range. Section Q's Eulerian nucleation field, if it is ever needed, is a
  separate mechanism and is not implemented here.
