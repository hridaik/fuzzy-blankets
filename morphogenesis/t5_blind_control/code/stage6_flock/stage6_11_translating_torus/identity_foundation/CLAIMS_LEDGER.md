## D. Newly discovered, not in the mandate's original list

1. **Candidate detection itself (`detect_69.propose`, Louvain) is not exactly permutation-invariant** — a small (≤2%, 1–2 birds on 2/10 candidates) but real and traced-to-cause violation (Louvain's node-visitation-order-dependent local optimum). `INVARIANCE_AND_TRANSFER_6_11.md` §1.2. Not previously disclosed in `RESULTS_6_11.md`. Directly relevant to `IDENTITY_MODEL.md` §5/§9(5)'s duplicate-invariance requirement: the non-invariance starts at the detector, before any lineage logic runs.
2. **No Stage 6.11 inference component is rotation-invariant** (heading identity is absolute in the relational predictor's category index, not relative to a rotated frame). Confirmed defect, not previously claimed as fixed anywhere — but also not previously claimed as invariant, so this is a disclosed gap rather than a retracted overstatement.
3. **Online-buffer "adaptation" is net-harmful at every tested speed, including the trained speed** (subject to the sample-rate caveat in item G above). `INVARIANCE_AND_TRANSFER_6_11.md` §2 Finding 4, self-described there as "not anticipated going in." A live open question for any future online-refit design in the new instrument, not a settled one.
4. **`B_causal` (task-neutral one-step causal interface) is computed, logged, and reported, but structurally never consumed by actuator selection** in the original pipeline (`LINEAGE_FORENSICS_6_11.md` §7) — a distinct defect from the privileged-pool leak (item A6): even a perfectly blind, perfectly estimated `B_causal` would not currently change which birds get forced, because `select_actuators` never reads it.
5. **Synergy is negative or negligible everywhere measured, including at the one seed (504) with genuine positive individual authority** (`AUTHORITY_AND_SET_EFFECT_AUDIT.md` §3: beam-search actuator set shows synergy of −0.043, cancelling ~70% of the summed individual effect). Relevant to any future Phase 4 controller-contract design: multi-actuator sets should not be assumed additive.
6. **Seed 504 has simultaneously the cleanest lineage tracking (single uncontested hypothesis throughout, zero duplicate-hypothesis steps) AND the only seed with real, true-parent-grounded, statistically robust authority — and still fails to turn under every tested controller.** Triangulated from `LINEAGE_FORENSICS_6_11.md` §6, `AUTHORITY_AND_SET_EFFECT_AUDIT.md` §3, and `FIVE_SEED_CAUSAL_ADJUDICATION.md`'s seed 504 section. This decouples "clean instrumentation" and "real authority" from "achieves the task" — a caution against assuming that fixing identity tracking alone (this pass's own project) will produce control success. **The new instrument built here is necessary, not sufficient, for eventual control success.**

## F. Visualization findings

**Done — see `VIZ_RECON.md` for the full writeup.** Summary: heading
glyphs were never removed from a single maintained renderer; two separate
files exist for two separate purposes. `figures/translating_collective_611.html`
(the actual `RESULTS_6_11.md` §8 deliverable) has working, correctly-
rotated triangle heading glyphs, confirmed by direct code read
(`drawBird`, `Math.atan2`) and by three fresh Playwright screenshots taken
this pass — but the glyphs are too small (`r=2.2–3.4`px) to be
individually legible at 400-bird world-view density, and there is no
zoom/follow view (confirmed absent by grep). `audit/viz_6_11b.html`/
`viz_6_11b_final.html` (the 6.11B tracker-comparison tool the six
pre-existing baseline screenshots were taken from) draws every bird as a
plain dot and never reads heading at all — confirmed by reading its
entire `draw()` function. This second file is almost certainly the source
of the "still looks jumpy" impression: no heading, no trail, so an
identity switch shows up only as an abrupt color change. Both files are
preserved unchanged; Phase 3 must reconcile conventions from both rather
than picking one.
