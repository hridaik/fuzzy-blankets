## Headline answers
1. **Does the theory hold (T1–T5)?** **Yes for T1–T4, partly for T5.** T1: isolated cells relax at 0.1003 (2r = 0.1). T2: l* = ±2.2920 (theory 2.2924), the same in every cell (ratiometric ⇒ independent of connectivity). T3: the simulated bath sweep jumps between 0.6 and 0.7 (critical value 0.636), stays switched when the bath is removed, and mirrors for the opposite bath. T4: structure is bit-identical to the sham twin under any memory intervention, deterministic and CRN-noisy (needs the sham to run the same traced program). T5: durability is excellent (0/20 body switches in 4·10⁵ tu) and the lifetime of N-cell groups grows exponentially with N, but with ≈ 30 % of the collective Kramers exponent (0.31 vs 1.06 per cell at σ_h = 0.7); at σ_h = 1.0 the first-sign-change criterion saturates (PROVISIONAL explanation: range-limited coupling lets a sub-group nucleate).
2. **Is there a movement-free, identity-preserving switch; dose; location dependence?** **Yes.** Memory-secretion light on a disc flips the state in both directions with structure bit-identical (no cell moves), the reporter following the mean ρ crossing by ≈ 2 tu. Dose = amplitude × duration × lit cells: at 4/g (6.7 tu) the best disc (place 10, 8 cells, amplitude 8.2) needs **440** vs **450** for whole-body light (only 2/24 discs beat it; the median disc 582, the worst 3,733); at 16/g (26.7 tu) best **233** (place 8, 9 cells, amplitude 1.0) vs 285 whole-body (14/24 discs beat it; median 260, worst 1,387). **No threshold at 1/g** (a single pulse can move l by at most ≈ g·dur ≈ 1 < l* = 2.29, tested to amplitude 2,000). Location dependence is strong and explained by kernel connectivity of the lit disc (Spearman with threshold amplitude −0.999 / −0.98; with dose −0.92 / −0.94): the corner places are the most expensive (8.5× / 6× best-to-worst). Under noise, at 1.2 × threshold: 59/60 and 54/60 (4/g), 120/120 (16/g); matched-dose wrong-location 0/120; sham bit-identical. A switched state persisted 2,000 tu in 8/8 long checks.
3. **How do fragments and fusions resolve the memory?** Fragments keep the state (cut halves: ρ = 0.91 for 1,500 tu deterministically; 12/12 fragments under noise); small groups decay on the quorum curve (2 cells: 238 tu, 12 cells: 5,852 tu at σ_h = 0.7). Newcomers adopt the group state within 2 tu (24/24), and the state survives complete turnover of the cells (serial replacement: 24 new ids, all in the original state, 4/4 — while structure is lost 0/4 complete). Fusion a+b: no systematic winner (a/b symmetric); bodies that stay distinct keep their own state with a persistent domain wall; strong overlap (offset 2) erases the wall in 50–400 tu (a 5/8, b 3/8); structure never merges the two bodies.
4. **Is the blind package ready?** **Yes** — `morphogenesis/testbed_blind_v2/`: 513 runs × 4 files (O1, O2, O3a, O3b), 260 natural dishes (1,040 decorrelated frames per state), 39 CRN triplets of identity events, 120 switch runs (below/at/above threshold, wrong location, sham; 6 centres incl. best and worst, 2 durations, both directions), 16 other-actuator runs; opaque labels; leak check PASS; split manifest by body / condition / actuator location. Hidden tier and mapping outside the package. The package was rebuilt once after a bug (the stress "twin" arm also received the operation) was caught by `tests/test_package.py`.

## Deviations and choices to review
* σ_h = 0.4 from the strict reading "every cell's std(l_i) ≥ 0.3" (median std 0.43, min 0.398).
* Memory-light ramps min(5, dur/4); H3 trials use release 20/g; **reporter scored over the last 10/g of the release** (it lags ρ by ≈ 2 tu); the first scoring pass (whole release) is kept in `data/superseded/` — it shifted the 4/g whole-body dose 780 → 450.
* Group (quorum) lifetime definition: first sign change of the mean l; at σ_h = 0.4 almost nothing flips within 10⁴ tu, so the curve was measured at σ_h = 0.7 (and 1.0).
* The G1 plasticity target is not improved: the slowest non-neutral mode is still the twin-exchange mode, 0.0089 (hidden covariance computation).
* H4b: single variant tried; not a general negative.
* Package contents beyond the spec: other-actuator decoy runs; per-run `fov` (30 for dish merges).
* Not done: persistence beyond 2,000 tu after switching (only 8 long checks); observer-side analyses (out of scope).
