# OPEN_QUESTIONS.md — testbed v3

1. **Quorum exponent.** The measured lifetime exponent per added cell (0.31 at σ_h = 0.7) is ≈ 30 % of the collective-Kramers value 2ΔU/σ_h² (1.06). The proposed explanation (a sub-group nucleus flips and spreads; range-limited coupling) is untested. A nucleation-size measurement (flip m of N cells and see whether they recruit the rest) would test it. At σ_h = 1.0 the "first sign change of the mean" criterion saturates (≈ 100 tu for all N): a better lifetime definition (e.g. sustained sign change, ≥ 50 tu) is needed there.
2. **No threshold at 1/g.** One pulse cannot exceed Δl ≈ g·dur ≈ 1 < l* = 2.29. Is a minimum duration ≈ l*/g (≈ 3.8 tu) the intended reading of "durations 1/g, 4/g, 16/g"? The 1/g column of the dose table is empty by theory, not by lack of amplitude (tested to 2000).
3. **Dose–connectivity relation.** Threshold amplitude falls with the kernel connectivity of the lit disc (Spearman ≈ −1). A parameter-free prediction of the threshold from W_i and the disc geometry (a mean-field bistability calculation with a local source) was not made.
4. **Noise-limited switch at 4/g, 1.2× threshold:** 59/60 and 54/60. The deficit is in a few noise realisations; whether 1.5× removes it (and the dose at which success is ≥ 99 %) was not measured.
5. **Fusion.** Which state wins depends on geometry and noise (a 5/8, b 3/8 at strong overlap); there is no label-level winner because a and b are symmetric. A sub-dish asymmetry (different cell number, an extra cell type) would be required to make a systematic winner exist.
6. **Structure still fails on serial replacement (0/4), cut across the body (±y), and fusion (no merge).** The G1 structure module is unchanged by design; H4b (attenuated place sensing, one variant) fails completeness. Other attenuation variants (e.g. also attenuating the action target) were not tried.
7. **Reporter scoring rule.** The reporter lags ρ by ≈ 2 tu; I scored it over the second half of the release. Different rules change the 4/g thresholds by ≈ 25 % (first pass: whole-body 780 → 450; best 520 → 440).
8. **Persistence beyond 20/g.** See SWITCH_V3.md (long persistence runs in `data/h3_persist.json`).
