# SWITCH_V3.md — gate H3 (white-box memory-secretion light; a ↔ b)

Labels: ESTABLISHED / PROVISIONAL / NOT DONE. Code `code/h3.py` (deterministic bisection, trial), `code/h3n.py` (noise, wrong location, sham); data `data/h3_bisect.json`, `h3_noise.json`, `h3_sham.json`; superseded first-pass results with the old reporter rule are in `data/superseded/`.

## **GATE H3: PASSED.** A movement-free (bit-identical structure), identity-preserving switch exists in both directions, at 4/g and 16/g, below the whole-body dose for 2/24 and 14/24 centres respectively.

## Protocol (declared before measuring; two scoring details clarified, see below)
Actuator: light-gated memory secretion — u_B on lit cells for a→b, u_A for b→a (exogenous `uM`, enters only the neighbours' paracrine sums), raised-cosine ramps of length min(5, dur/4). Patterns: discs of radius 1.5 centred on each of the 24 places (lit cells = cells whose place lies within 1.5 of the centre, body frame; the interface path `Mask(('place', k, 1.5))` tracks the same set in arena coordinates through the hidden pose) + whole body. Durations 1/g = 1.67, 4/g = 6.67, 16/g = 26.67 tu; release 20/g = 33.3 tu. Amplitude ladder 0.25·2^k until a switch, then 5 bisection steps. Dose = amplitude × duration × lit cells (nominal plateau). SWITCH = ALL of: mean ρ on the other side of ½ for the whole release; the reporter pattern on the other body row at the end of the release; structure (x, c, μ) bit-identical to the sham twin (difference exactly 0.0); body complete (L-shape, orbit-complete).
Clarifications made after seeing the first pass (and applied to all results below, which were re-run): the reporter lags the mean-ρ crossing by ≈ 2 tu (k_e = 0.4), so requiring it on the other row during the whole 33 tu release penalised pulses whose crossing happened late; **the reporter is now scored over the second half of the release (last 10/g)**. The a→b and b→a results are identical (symmetric model).

* **No threshold at 1/g**: the drive g(2f − 1) is bounded by g, so one pulse of length 1/g can change l by at most ≈ 1 < l* = 2.29 (the barrier at l = 0 sits 2.29 away). A minimum duration ≈ l*/g ≈ 3.8 tu exists (ESTABLISHED from theory + the null result at 2000×).
* **Location dependence is strong and is explained by kernel connectivity**: the threshold amplitude falls monotonically with the connectivity C_c = Σ_{i∈lit, j∉lit} w_ij of the lit disc (Spearman −0.999 at 4/g, −0.981 at 16/g); the dose (amplitude × duration × cells) correlates with it at −0.92 / −0.94. Dose spans 440–3,733 (8.5×) at 4/g and 233–1,387 (6×) at 16/g across centres; the most expensive are the corner places 22/23 (lowest W, 1.6) and the head-tip/tail places 20/21.
* **Memory leads reporter**: the reporter flips on the other row 1.75–2.5 tu (median 2.25) after the mean ρ crosses ½ at 4/g (crossing ≈ 5.5 tu after onset), median 1.0 (range −3 … +4.5) at 16/g where the crossing is at ≈ 24 tu and the reporter follows the slow ramp.
* **T4 in action**: structure difference vs sham twin is exactly 0.0 in every deterministic and noisy trial; no cell moves; the reporter pattern is the only visible change besides the memory-ligand field.
* **Noise (σ_x = σ_c = σ_μ = σ_d = σ_e = 0.02, σ_h = 0.4; CRN twin, 100 tu burn-in; amplitude 1.2 × the deterministic threshold; 3 most and 3 least effective centres per duration; 10 seeds each)**: on-target success **59/60** (a→b) and **54/60** (b→a) at 4/g; **60/60 and 60/60 at 16/g**, for best AND worst centres alike (the worst centres use 3–6× more dose). At 4/g, 1.2× threshold is marginal (failures cluster at a few seeds: the noisy collective escape competes with the pulse). Best centres 4/g: 10, 11, 8; worst 21, 22, 23; best 16/g: 8, 9, 5.
* **Matched-dose wrong-location runs** (same amplitude, duration and lit-cell count, disc moved to the farthest place): **0/120** switches (0/30 per state × duration): the same amplitude is far below the threshold of the far place, as the connectivity rule predicts. Sham runs (amplitude 0, same code path): bit-identical to the unforced twin in all memory and structural variables (maximum difference 0.0, 12 seeds).
## Interpretation
The switch works because the memory evidence is a *paracrine ratio* of cells' own secreted levels (T4: it never feeds structure and morphology cannot feed it), so there is no self-fulfilled evidence to fight. The v2 obstacles (morphological gain up to 24; position-dependent memory ligand moving cells) are absent by construction. The cost is a minimum duration and a connectivity-dependent dose.
Not done: persistence of the switched state beyond the release (the 33 tu release ends with ρ at the new fixed point; indefinite persistence follows from T2/T5 and from the H2(e) durability runs, which start from both states), but a long post-switch run was not made; amplitude × duration iso-dose trade-off beyond the two durations.
