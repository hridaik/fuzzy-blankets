## A2 V_body as implemented (`t5_blind_control/code/t5mon/monitor.py`, `geom.py`)
* **Material** (l. 28–31, 48–49): id set vs the first frame; `mat_ok` iff nothing lost/arrived and the set is unchanged; `material_J` = |ids₀∩ids|/|ids₀∪ids| (whole-dish id set, **not** the collective's member set: a cut or an extrusion leaves it at 1).
* **Geometry** (l. 32–34, 50–54; `geom.py` 8–18, `describe.py` 13–23): `geo_ok` = 24 cells ∧ one component at r_link 1.6 ∧ MST bridge ≤ **1.4000** ∧ |s1/s1_base − 1| ≤ **4.875 %** ∧ |s2/s2_base − 1| ≤ **6.642 %**, with s1, s2 the sd along the principal axes and the base the median of the first `nb` = 10 frames. Raw per-frame values, no smoothing. Bounds = 99 % quantile of 600-tu window maxima on 12 calibration dishes × 1.15 (`MONITOR_CONFIG.json`), a 95 % level kept as `geometry_warn` (l. 55).
* **Pattern** (l. 23, 25, 51, 56): 14 state-independent features, 10-tu(5-frame) causal median, dz = max |median − baseline|/sd_within ≤ 4.835. Not part of V_body.
* **Events** (l. 30–34): LOSS, ARRIVAL, SPLIT (≥ 2 components of ≥ 3 cells), EXTRUSION (any extra component, no loss).
* **V_body** (l. 59) = cumulative `vb_ok ∧ mat_ok ∧ geo_ok` (latches at the first violating frame). **V_body_conservative** (l. 60) = `vb_ok ∧ ¬event_flag`. **They are logically identical**: LOSS/ARRIVAL already violate `mat_ok`, and SPLIT/EXTRUSION imply `ncomp > 1`, which already violates `geo_ok`; so the "conservative" flag never adds information (observed: equal in all 428 episodes).
* **Window/frame mismatch (ESTABLISHED, code).** `W = window_fast_tu / dt_frame` and `nb` are in *frames*; the config was calibrated at 2-tu frames (`dt_frame` 2.0) but the controller ran at **1 tu** (live logs spacing 1.0). Hence the live "10-tu median" is 5 tu and the baseline is 10 tu (documents say 20). This does not touch V_body's raw geometry statistic but doubles the number of frames the latch sees per calibrated window and halves the state smoothing.
* **Is the natural false-alarm rate the monitor's or the body's?** (`data/a2_falsealarm.json`) Observed V_body false in 3/62 dishes (4.8 %). Noise-free, the true bridge exceeds 1.4000 in 1/62 (6001: 1.74); the other two sit 0.007 and 0.052 below the bound and are pushed over by position noise (observed − true: median −0.003, 95th percentile +0.027 per dish). A 3-frame median of the observed bridge would have removed both noise alarms (1 alarm remains). So: **a property of the monitor** (raw max statistic + latch + a bound that natural true maxima approach: true max-bridge quantiles 50/90/95/99 % = 1.05/1.06/1.17/1.53) **plus a real, rare body fluctuation**; it is not a loss of identity in any case.

## A3 Regret (ESTABLISHED for the protocols defined; `data/a3_regret.json`, `code/regret.py`)
Per dish, from the engine state at the first action (t = 20 tu), scale s common to the amplitudes of a protocol; minimal dose = s* × dose used. Deterministic = noise-free log-bisection (<0.5 %); noisy = median over 10 CRN keys of per-key 5-step bisection within [0.6, 1.5] × s_det (lower end can clip; clipping not counted). Regret = dose used / minimal dose (60 dishes):
| arm | regret, deterministic: median [95 % CI] (IQR) | regret, noisy 50 %: median [CI] (IQR) |
|---|---|---|

| controller vs one 10-tu pulse at its first disc | 1.08 [1.06, 1.11] (1.03–1.24) | — |
| **fixed pulse** (20 tu, amp 2.0) | **1.36** [1.35, 1.36] | **1.54** [1.52, 1.58] |
| **whole body, same amplitude** | **2.30** [2.29, 2.33] | **2.86** [2.77, 2.95] |
| whole body, dose-matched | 0.64 (fails by construction) | 0.80 |
| random location | 0.65 | — |
Reading: the controller spends close to the minimum for its own train; this is partly by construction (it stops as soon as a train works, so its used dose tracks its own threshold). Against the noise-averaged threshold it uses 12 % more than needed, the fixed pulse 54 %, the whole body 2.9×, i.e. the controller is 1.4× closer to optimal than the fixed pulse and 2.5× than the whole body — but all 60 dishes of the fixed pulse succeed because its regret is stable (IQR 1.48–1.61). Whole-body dose-matched sits at 0.80 of its noisy threshold, which is why it fails 59/60. **Calibration check:** expected success from the 10 CRN keys at the dose used — fixed 1.00 (observed 0.98), whole 1.00 (0.98), controller 0.74 (observed 0.93): the controller's 7 % misses are not predicted by threshold noise alone because its train is adaptive. Direction: median noisy regret 1.17 (a→b) vs 1.01 (b→a).

## A5 Actuators (ESTABLISHED; `data/a5_actuators.json`)
Screening (dev seeds 5001 a, 5002 b; centre disc, 10 tu, amp 1/5/25) vs the offline untreated twin: **L3 = memory-A secretion, L4 = memory-B secretion**: structure bit-identical (max |ΔX| = |ΔC| = |ΔMU| = 0.000), memory only. One direction each: L4 flips a (Δρ −0.79) and does nothing on b; L3 flips b (+0.77) and does nothing on a, at every amplitude — the A (or B) ligand fraction saturates at 1 (0) when the dish is already in that state. **L1 = migration gain**: harmless at amp 1–5 (|ΔX| ≤ 0.04), body destroyed at 25 (5–7 groups). **L2 = structural secretion**: changes codes from amp 1 (|ΔC| 0.45, |ΔX| 0.8, still one group), splits at amp ≥ 5 (2 groups). **L5 = receptor gain**: |ΔC| 2.0 at amp 1, diverges numerically (positions 10⁷) at 5. T5's "L1/L2/L5 only change pattern or damage the body" is **confirmed**, with the nuance that they act on structure (positions move too, even for pattern-level doses); memory moves only as a side effect of breaking the body (Δρ ≤ 0.25 at the destroying doses).
