# GROUND_TRUTH_EXPORTS.md — T2f hidden-tier exports

Code `code/exports.py` (`export_run`, `load_tier`, `influence_norms`); test `tests/test_engine.py::test_hidden_tier_requires_audit`. ESTABLISHED.

## Layout (per run `<name>`)
* `<name>_obs.npz` — OBSERVABLE tier: `t`, `X` (T,n,2) positions, `C` (T,n,nc) secreted signals, `types` (T,n) colour type 1–4 derived from the secretion.
* `<name>_hid.npz` — HIDDEN tier: `alive` (T,n) (all True: turnover is OFF), `cell_id` (permanent), `membership` (all alive cells), `component` / `n_components` (connected components at radius 1.6 = declared geometric split test), `extruded` (cell with no neighbour within 1.6), `role` = argmax place belief (T,n), `belief` (T,n,n) full place softmax, `planB` (T,n) plan-B belief (zeros when K = 1), `free_energy` (T,n) per-cell, `influence_frames`, `influence_norm` (F,n,n).
* **Influence relation** (ground truth, BRIDGE C1/C2): `influence_norm[f][i,j]` = Frobenius norm of the autodiff Jacobian block ∂(flow of cell i: dx_i, dc_i, dmu_i, dzeta_i)/∂(state of cell j: x_j, c_j, mu_j, zeta_j), i ≠ j, at 4 frames per run. The kernel exp(-κ d) is non-zero for every pair, so influence is graded; contact (distance) and influence are exported separately (positions are in the obs tier) so that later stages can compare them. Full Jacobians are available on demand from `eng.jac_flat`.
* **Loader**: `load_tier(path, 'obs')` always works; `load_tier(path, 'hid')` raises `PermissionError` unless `audit=True` is passed.
The OBSERVABLE viewer builds physically omit target / plan belief / role (checked in tests).
Cells are material and permanent in the testbed, so material membership is trivial ground truth; role (slot) is the quantity a later identity analysis must infer.


## Update (Testbed v1)
* **Alive mask, permanent ids, events** (IDENTITY_EVENTS.md): `World.frames` carry `alive` and `cell_id` per frame; `World.events` / `Experiment.hidden()['events']` log remove / insert / replace (with the replaced id) / extrude / cut / fuse with time and parameters (times are engine-absolute: subtract 1e4 for run time).
* **Influence exports** (`data/influence_L.npz`, `influence_R.npz`, `code/influence_forms.py`): autodiff Jacobian block norms ∂(flow of cell i)/∂(state of cell j) at 4 declared frames per form (settled adult at t = 1e4 and after 100, 200, 300 further time units under noise), with the distance matrix and the kernel weights exp(−d). Over cell pairs the influence norm correlates 0.96 with the kernel weight (frame 0); median influence of the nearest-10 % pairs 2.3 vs 0.026 beyond distance 5. So in this model the true influence relation is almost entirely the geometric kernel (no FOV or directed gate), which is the reference for BRIDGE C1/C2.
* **Analytic stationary covariance** (`data/lna_L.npz`, `lna_R.npz`, `lna_summary.json`, `code/lyap.py`): linear-noise (Lyapunov) covariance of (x, c, mu) of the 24 cells at the L and R adult states from the autodiff Jacobian and the diagonal noise covariance (sigma_x = sigma_c = 0.02, sigma_mu = 0). **Caveats (PROVISIONAL):** (i) the rigid-motion modes (2–3 neutral) have no stationary law and are projected out; (ii) the saturated belief modes have rates −1e-5…−1e-4 and a few have numerically positive real parts (79 modes with Re > 1e-7 at L, 62 at R; |Re| ≲ 1e-5, i.e. the relaxed state is not an exact fixed point), so the full covariance (position variance per coordinate 0.063 (L), 0.67 (R)) is dominated by modes that are not at a stationary state within any observed time; (iii) the covariance of the FAST subspace (modes faster than 1e-2 per time unit; positions variance per coordinate 2.8e-3 (L), 1.2e-3 (R)) is the quantity relevant on observation windows ≲ 100 time units, and agrees to within a factor 1.5–3.5 with the detrended simulated fluctuations (variance per coordinate ≈ 8e-4). Use the fast-subspace covariance for the BRIDGE M1 leakage, with this caveat.
