# OPEN_QUESTIONS.md (testbed T1/T2)

1. **Origin of the factor 1.5 in D0.** The action prior/sensory ratio e⁻⁴/1.5 was fitted to the oracle's stationarity; it presumably comes from the generalised-coordinate precision structure in `spm_ADEM` (`dE.da`, `iG`) — not derived. PROVISIONAL. The position residual of the engine at the oracle state is 5.6e-4 (secretion 1.7e-8), i.e. the x-channel prior differs from the c-channel by ~0.06 % — unexplained.
2. **Is the oracle's period-2 cycle a scheme artefact?** The engine never cycles for DH/DT steps, but those were step onsets from one body, not the oracle's quasi-static sweeps; a sweep protocol in the engine (and the missing generalised-coordinate / delay terms) would settle it.
3. **Plan B.** Why is the 24-cell two-headed body not an attractor while A and the chiral body are? Candidates: 16 same-type cells, fingerprint twins across the two heads, weak graded channel. Test: change the recoded region to a different type than the front head; scan the axial-morphogen amplitude; compute the Jacobian's unstable eigenvectors at B.
4. **Mixture-prediction memory cannot create a barrier on shared slots** (TESTBED/MEMORY analysis, PROVISIONAL). Options for a redesign: recode a larger fraction (> 40 %) or all slots; make plan B's slot geometry differ everywhere (rescale); non-linear plan prediction (sigmoid of belief); or an explicit cell-autonomous bistable switch whose product is the memory ligand. All depart from the specification; decide before building.
5. **Development.** Self-assembly from near-uniform beliefs fails even for the published-style absolute-position model at 16–24 cells (0/50 for 24 cells); the vanilla 16-cell landscape was already multi-class. Is a seeded start an acceptable "default development" for later stages? (The testbed uses it.)
6. **Neutral belief modes.** At log π_prior = −10 the slowest belief rates are ~1e-4; belief-block quantities barely relax, and information-theoretic estimators will see a nearly frozen hidden state with little temporal variation. Decide the trade-off between crisp roles (T2b) and relaxing hidden variables before any identity/partition analysis.
7. Natural ensembles: kind B members are not a valid plan-B reference (not an attractor); the A and chiral ensembles have 12 + 6 members only, and belief variables do not decorrelate within a run.
8. Chirality pulses: non-monotone outcomes; one body, two signals, one duration; the L→defect threshold was not bisected; the mirror switch may exist at longer durations or other radii.
9. **Viewer never opened in a browser** (no browser/node in the session); only structural checks (script bracket balance; OBSERVABLE builds contain no target/plan/role). Open at least one file before relying on it.
10. Stochastic integrator: no strong/weak order measurement; noise only on x and c (not on beliefs), so spontaneous plan/belief switching is not even in the model at the operating noise.

## Update (follow-up instruction, Testbed v1)
12. **Two-plan memory model PARKED — recorded negative result.** Plan B (two-headed) is not an attractor of the relational model, even stand-alone (0/78 seeded draws, unstable Jacobian), and the plan-mixture model with a memory ligand showed no double well for any coupling tried (MEMORY_CALIBRATION.md). Per the review decision it is not iterated further. The control target of the programme is the chiral body's mirror switch (L <-> R); see SITUS_CALIBRATION.md.
13. **Mirror switch not available (Gate S failed).** Plasticity (belief relaxation) and sharp stable beliefs conflict: the slowest non-neutral belief rate equals k_mu·pi_prior, so crisp bodies (pi_prior ≤ e^-8) relax in ~2 600 time units, and neither belief noise (dissolves the body), a natural-gradient belief flow (destabilises it), higher sensory precision, nine physical actuator families (1728 runs), a gradient search (36 optimisations) nor a direct fate-bias channel (184 runs) produced a complete R body. Candidate redesigns (not tried): a per-cell committed-identity variable with a genuine bistable switch; belief dynamics with a floor on off-slot belief (e.g. belief mixing with a uniform component of 1e-2) so that logit gradients do not vanish; a body in which L and R differ by position (a real geometric flip) rather than by an exchange of 8 cell types.
14. **Replacement/turnover repair is poor** (9/24 single replacements repaired; serial replacement 0/6 survive). Is that a property of the saturated beliefs (13) or of the relational field?
15. **Decorrelation time ~150–200 time units** for body-frame positions (integrated autocorrelation 160–190), set by soft modes; hidden belief variables do not decorrelate in any run. Information-theoretic estimators will be sample-limited by the number of bodies.
