## Truth definitions (hidden tier) — as implemented (`an4.py`, `world4.py`)
ORGANISM INTACT = (i) no LOSS/ARRIVAL, all original cells alive; (ii) one connected component at 1.6; (iii) place completeness by argmax q, orbit-aware as v3 (θ = 0 orbits: mirror twins with energy gap < ln 9/4 are one orbit) — "exactly once" is read per orbit; (iv) mean neighbour-set Jaccard (radius 1.6) vs the baseline set (the settled body at the start of the trial) ≥ τ_J = **0.9332**, frozen in `data/intact_threshold.json`: τ_J = min of the 1st percentiles of the *window-minimum* statistic (min of the mean Jaccard within a 330-tu window) over 576 natural windows (P1 0.9332) and over 120 clean deterministic switch runs (P1 1.0). The frame-wise reading of the percentile (0.9826) was rejected because it flags ~1 % of ordinary natural frames. For dish-merge runs the flag is per original organism (cells 0–23 and 24–47 separately). SHAPE = signed tail angle (Procrustes body frame, head→hinge vs hinge→tail centroid, + toward limb side). STATE = sign of mean ρ.

## Declared choices
* Orbits and the typed shape distance use the θ = 0 orbits / the better of the a and b templates; a "complete" body is judged on place fill, not on the θ = 0 typed distance.
* Clamped-ρ runs are seeded in the template of the clamped side (ρ ≥ ½ → a, else b).

* Light damage: u_i = max over |uM|, |ext|, |rg|, |mig| of the already-windowed control entries of cell i (pipettes and baths are not lights). Per-dish parameters are runtime entries of the control tuple (`hp = (g, γ, α)`, 3 numbers) so one compiled program serves all dishes; `rho_fix` is control entry 12.
* The Jacobian block ∂l̇/∂(x, c, μ) at α = 0, γ = 0: exactly 0.0 for ∂/∂c, ∂/∂μ, ∂/∂ū; ∂l̇/∂x is not exactly 0 — it is the inherited O(ε) dependence of the ratio f on positions through the weights (exactly ~1e-12 at ε = 0 and uniform ρ, < 5e-3 at ε = 10⁻³); with ρ clamped, structure is bit-identical under 12 memory interventions (deterministic and noisy).
