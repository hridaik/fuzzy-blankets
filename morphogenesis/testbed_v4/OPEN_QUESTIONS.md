# OPEN_QUESTIONS.md — testbed v4

1. **Why is there no adaptivity gap, and what design would create one?** (ADAPTIVITY_GAP.md) The fixed-time durations make the middle-disc threshold nearly dish-independent (8/g_med: ×1.05 over 40 dishes) and χ (damage onset in γ·amplitude units) is independent of κ_g, so only the damage factor spreads the window (×2–8). Untested: durations in each dish's own 1/g (threshold ×6), a κ_g-dependent damage law, a heavier-tailed damage factor, per-dish disc geometry. The "PROVISIONAL" reason for the flat 8/g threshold has not been tested (e.g. by varying σ_h or the paracrine range).
2. **Per-dish damage onset was not searched** for the 40 dishes (interpolated χ(κ_g), validated on 8 dishes; widened levels only on the middle disc). A full per-dish search would remove the declared deviation but cost ≈ 60 core-hours.
3. **θ = 30° is not realisable from the seeded starts** (14/16) while θ = 20° is; at 20° one clamped start (seed 5, ρ = 0) is a stable orbit-complete mis-shaped body (tail twins 20/21 exchanged, −7.4° instead of −21.4°) that the orbit-aware completeness test and the neighbour Jaccard do not flag. How often does this defect arise from natural starts, and should "complete" include a shape criterion?
4. **Strict place completeness is unusable** (twin places have near-equal beliefs: strict fails in 64–69 % of healthy noisy frames); the orbit-aware definition cannot see twin-exchange defects in the shape. A twin-resolving criterion (e.g. the tail angle against the settled angle) would close the hole but was not part of the specified ORGANISM INTACT.
5. **The neighbour-Jaccard threshold at link radius 1.6 detects tearing, not shape change** (a 20° tail swing leaves Jaccard at 1.0), and its natural false-positive rate is non-zero (3/32 natural dishes in 3,000 tu; one CRN stream flags all six untreated twins of dish 607 as not intact). τ_J was calibrated on the window-minimum statistic because the frame-wise percentile (0.9826) would flag ~1 % of ordinary frames; the spec's wording ("the 1st percentile of that statistic") admits both readings.
6. **Noisy switch success under the frozen 20 % shape criterion is low (49 % (531/1,080) of the noisy trials at 1.2 × the deterministic threshold; memory state flipped in 97.6 %).** The criterion compares the end-of-release tail angle with the deterministic settled angle; noise lowers the mean angle (⟨2ρ−1⟩ < tanh(l*/2)), so most failures are the shape criterion, not the memory. Is the reference the deterministic angle or the noisy one?
7. **The dial's self-field term is not small for a cell without neighbours**: the isolated-cell forgetting rate is 0.16 / 0.33 (not 2r = 0.10) with a biased fixed point at l = −1.4 / −2.1 for φ_max 0.4 / 0.8. What happens to a far-extruded cell, a very small fragment or an isolated cell in a dish in v4 was not examined (V6 extrusion cells that did not rejoin were within paracrine range).
8. **The lock condition (φ ≥ 1 at large bath) was not tested**; the declared variants have φ̄ ≤ 0.114 and per-cell φ_k ≤ 0.49. A body-level test needs φ_max ≈ 4–9.
9. **V6 covered only the primary variant and state-a starts**; replacement of state-b cells, cut of state-b bodies and the φ0 / φ0.8 variants were not run.
10. **Dish-merge flags:** the hidden ORGANISM INTACT flag is defined per original organism for merge runs; interpenetrating clusters can remain "intact" by that definition (cross-cluster neighbours are ignored).
11. **Low-κ_g dishes switch spontaneously** (1 of 32 natural 3,000-tu dishes; V2d's 0/60 is for the median dish only): what is the κ_g below which the memory does not last 10⁴ tu at σ_h 0.4?
12. **Deferred flags (per-dish swap of the two memory lights; body rotation) were not implemented**, only declared OFF.
13. Wrong-location controls do not exist for the tail-end disc (nothing is harder to switch).
