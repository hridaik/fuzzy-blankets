## Headline answers
1. **Do both shapes hold?** Yes at θ = 20° (not at the specified 30°): both clamped shapes complete from 16/16 seeded starts and last 5 × 5,000 tu, and the free memory state holds each shape for 10 × 10,000 tu per state and variant with no spontaneous switch (median dish). One mis-shaped stable defect (tail twins exchanged) exists at ρ = 0, seed 5; low-κ_g dishes do switch spontaneously (1/32 natural 3,000-tu dishes).
2. **Does shape follow memory without tearing?** Yes for a clean pulse: in 180/180 deterministic conditions the tail swings over with at most 1.3 % of neighbour pairs changed and no cell lost; the shape angle follows (2ρ − 1) and lags or leads the mean-ρ crossing by −8 … +24 tu depending on which cells flip. Tearing appears only above the damage onset (wrong places of the lit cells) and **always at the tail-end disc**, where the damage onset is 0.26–0.41 of the switch threshold. Under noise only 49 % of pulses at 1.2 × the deterministic threshold pass the frozen 20 % shape criterion although 97.6 % flip the memory.
3. **Does the feedback dial behave as predicted (g_eff, lock condition)?** g_eff and l* yes (≤ 0.5 %), bath critical value yes (≤ 4 %, φ0.8 is 4 % low), hysteresis yes, cost on the switch dose +3…+26 %, flip order = increasing φ_k. The lock condition (φ ≥ 1) was not reached by any declared variant (φ̄ ≤ 0.11, φ_k ≤ 0.49) and is NOT DONE. The isolated-cell prediction (rate 2r) is **falsified** for α > 0.
4. **Is there a safe window, and does it move between dishes?** Yes at the middle (and limb-side) disc: 2.4–3.0 × at the median dish, open at every duration; **none** at the tail-end disc (and none for the head-end disc at 16/g for any dish). It moves between dishes through the switch threshold (κ_g) and the damage factor, but not through χ (independent of κ_g): at the middle disc the window stays open for all 40 dishes at 8/g_med at every level (at 4/16/32 g_med only 0.85–0.93 of the dishes at the widest population).
5. **Does the adaptivity gap exist?** **No, not in this design.** One fixed middle-disc pulse (≈ 16 tu, amplitude ≈ 2.5, or 4/g_med at amplitude 10 for the specified population) works for 100 % of 40 dishes at the specified population and after both widenings; the per-dish oracle gains only a dose factor ×1.3–1.6. V5 is reported as failed.

## Declared deviations and choices (all labelled where they arise)
* θ = 20° (fallback, because V1 failed at 30°); α, D_k recomputed (max D_k 6.304). "Target template's angle" in the SWITCH criterion = the settled tail angle of the target state in that dish (the nominal ±20° is unreachable by construction).
* ORGANISM INTACT (iii) is orbit-aware (strict argmax fill fails in 64–69 % of healthy noisy frames); τ_J = 0.9332 from the window-minimum statistic (the frame-wise percentile 0.9826 was rejected); the merge-run flag is per organism.
* V3 noise runs used 6 seeds per condition (not 10) and 1.2 × the deterministic threshold.
* V5: damage onset for the 40 dishes = exact χ on a κ_g grid, interpolated and validated on 8 dishes (error ≤ 4.3 %, within the 6 % search resolution); widened levels on the middle disc only; the second widening (κ_g [1.1, 7], factor [1/3, 3]) was declared before it was computed.
* V6 only for the primary variant and state-a starts. Deferred flags (light swap, body rotation) are declared OFF and not implemented.
* The data package and live environment were built although V5 failed.
