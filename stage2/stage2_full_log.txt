=== Coverage study (target 1-alpha=0.95) ===
NOTE ON COMPUTE BUDGET: the task suggests M>=500 bootstrap replicates and enough outer
datasets for credible coverage estimates. Coverage assessment requires a NESTED bootstrap
(R_outer observed datasets, each with its own M-replicate bootstrap), which is far more
expensive than Part C's unnested bias check. An initial run at R_outer=200, M_boot=500 was
observed to be dominated by raw random-number generation volume at n=5000 (M*n*d draws per
outer replicate) rather than by any avoidable inefficiency (a real einsum->matmul bottleneck
was found and fixed in bootstrap_replicates_cov, but did not fully resolve the runtime). We
therefore reduced to R_outer=100, M_boot=300 for tractability; R_outer=100 gives a binomial
coverage SE of ~2.2% at the 95% target, adequate to detect miscalibration >~5-6%. This is a
compute-budget reduction, not a tuning of results, and is reported per the instructions.
