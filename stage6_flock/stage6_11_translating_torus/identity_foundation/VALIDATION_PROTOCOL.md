# VALIDATION_PROTOCOL.md

Frozen BEFORE any synthetic-generator or tracker-implementation code is
written, per the mandate's requirement to separate tuning and held-out
seeds in advance and to score error tradeoffs rather than optimize a single
jump-count number. This document specifies WHAT will be measured and on
WHAT data; it contains no results (those belong in `VALIDATION_RESULTS.md`,
written only after implementation, once `MATHEMATICAL_CHECKS.md` has
passed its adversarial review — see `CLAIMS_LEDGER.md` for gate status).

## 1. Two independent data sources, never pooled into one score

1. **Synthetic event generator** — a NEW, independent generator (not
   `MovingFlock611`) that produces agent-level observations from KNOWN
   organizational trajectories/genealogy. Its purpose is to test the
   identity model against ground truth the real flock cannot provide.
   Two sub-modes, both required (per the mandate's explicit instruction
   not to judge the tracker only on data generated from its own
   assumptions):
   - **Matched-model calibration**: agents literally drawn from the §4
     wrapped-elliptical-mixture generative story, so the identity model's
     own assumptions hold exactly. Tests implementation correctness and
     calibration, not misspecification robustness.
   - **Out-of-model tests**: agents generated by a DIFFERENT mechanism
     that produces the same qualitative scenario (e.g. an actual small
     multi-agent flocking simulation for the "crossing" and "merger"
     scenarios, or a hand-authored non-elliptical, non-Gaussian shape for
     "elongated/disconnected regions") — deliberately violating the
     mixture's conditional-independence and elliptical-shape assumptions,
     to see how much the identity read-out degrades under
     misspecification rather than assuming zero degradation.
2. **Real 6.11 replay** — the five existing real online-control episodes
   (seeds 500–504, frozen, `code/run_online_control_611.py`'s own output).
   No ground truth exists for these; scoring here is necessarily
   comparative (v1 vs v2 vs new model vs a transparent baseline) and
   disagreement-reporting, never validated against an "answer."

**These two are never pooled into one aggregate score.** Synthetic results
establish calibration/identifiability under known truth; real replay
results establish which tracker's account of the SAME raw observations is
more defensible, with no privileged "ground truth" tracker (the mandate's
explicit prohibition on treating v2 as truth, `CLAIMS_LEDGER.md` item B/A).

## 2. Frozen seed/scenario split (tuning vs. held-out)

| split | scenario seeds | purpose | may be looked at while building the tracker? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **dev** | synthetic seeds 0–19 (both matched-model and out-of-model sub-modes) | threshold calibration, process-noise fitting (§8 of `IDENTITY_MODEL.md`'s `eta_c`/`eta_v`/`gamma`), debugging | YES |
| **held-out synthetic** | synthetic seeds 20–49 | primary reported synthetic metrics | NO — not inspected until the tracker implementation is frozen |
| **real replay** | the 5 existing seeds 500–504 | comparative real-data readout (§4 below) | N/A — these are fixed, already-recorded episodes; "looking" at them cannot leak into tracker design in the usual sense, but per-seed tuning of thresholds against these 5 outcomes is forbidden (the same discipline PLAN.md Section V rule 6 already states for thingness thresholds, extended here to the whole identity model) |

Any threshold, process-noise variance, or scale-stability tolerance
calibrated on dev seeds is FROZEN before the held-out synthetic run and
before the real replay — re-tuning after seeing held-out or real-replay
results is exactly the "do not make thresholds easier until examples pass"
violation the mandate names explicitly, and would invalidate the held-out
numbers retroactively.

## 3. Required synthetic scenarios (dev set built to cover all of these; held-out set independently re-samples the same scenario family with different seeds, not different scenario TYPES)

Directly enumerated by the mandate, each built as its own small,
inspectable generator configuration:

1. Two separate moving clumps (Case 1 baseline, `MATHEMATICAL_CHECKS.md`)
2. Crossing (Case 3/3b)
3. Temporary superposition (detector-merger sub-case of Case 4)
4. One observed blob from two still-distinct groups (Case 4 setup A)
5. Actual merger (Case 4 setup B)
6. Symmetric split (Case 5)
7. Growth (Case 6)
8. Contraction (mirror of Case 6)
9. Torus seam crossing (Case 9)
10. Missed detection (the "8-contrast" scenario in `MATHEMATICAL_CHECKS.md`)
11. Rapid but plausible motion (a high-`v` variant of Case 1, still inside
    the calibrated reachable set)
12. Complete constituent replacement (Case 7)
13. Destruction followed by a remote lookalike (Case 8)
14. Density change (a mass/`m` variation independent of shape)
15. Compact vs. elongated vs. disconnected regions (three shape variants
    of the same underlying scenario, to stress-test the elliptical-mixture
    misspecification specifically)
16. Exactly ambiguous encounters (Case 10)

Each scenario is generated at >=20 independent seeds in the dev split and
>=20 independent (disjoint) seeds in the held-out split, in BOTH matched
and out-of-model sub-modes where the scenario admits both (some, like
"torus seam crossing," are primarily a housekeeping check and may only
need the matched-model sub-mode).

## 4. Trackers compared, on both synthetic and real data

1. **v1** (`code/lineage_611.LineageTracker611`) — the current production
   tracker, unmodified.
2. **v2** (`audit/lineage_v2_611.LineageTrackerV2`) — the prior comparator,
   unmodified, run here for the first time against a validation protocol
   that treats it as a hypothesis under test, not as ground truth.
3. **Transparent baseline** — a NEW, deliberately simple motion+shape
   association tracker with an explicit no-match/unresolved alternative
   (e.g. nearest-candidate-by-centroid-displacement within a fixed
   reachable-set radius, else unresolved) — included specifically so that
   "the new joint model wins" is checked against a simple, honest
   alternative, not only against the two already-diagnosed-flawed priors.
4. **Proposed joint model** — the `IDENTITY_MODEL.md` specification, once
   implemented.

## 5. Metrics (error tradeoff, never a single jump-count scalar)

Per the mandate's explicit list, computed for every tracker x every
scenario x every seed, then aggregated with confidence intervals across
seeds (never a single point estimate presented as final):

- Correct object count; births/deaths (precision/recall against the known
  synthetic event log, where available); false tracks; missed tracks.
- Localization/extent error (`c_ell`, `S_ell` against known truth,
  synthetic only).
- Identity switches and trajectory fragmentation — using the
  assignment-based, time-weighted trajectory metric family from
  García-Fernández et al. (arXiv:2110.13444), per `IDENTITY_MODEL.md` §1
  item 5, NOT an ad hoc jump-count.
- Event precision/recall and reporting delay (time between a true event
  and the tracker's own report of it, synthetic only).
- Existence/association calibration (synthetic only, where truth is
  known): reliability curves — does a tracker's own reported
  `P(correct AND exists)` actually match empirical correctness frequency
  at that reported probability.
- Unresolved time and false-confident-assignment rate — the mandate's
  explicit anti-gaming metrics: a tracker that never detects, kills every
  track, or freezes a stale label must score badly here even if its
  jump/switch count looks good, and this is reported ALONGSIDE the
  switch-count metrics, on the same table, never as a separate footnote.
- Survival of tracking through real turns and constituent replacement
  (scenarios 7, 11 above) — does the tracker correctly NOT charge an event
  when the model says it shouldn't.
- Sensitivity to proposal duplication/order and observation scale — direct
  application of `MATHEMATICAL_CHECKS.md`'s Case 2 and the cross-cutting
  checks, run as an automated test over every scenario, not just the
  isolated toy case.

## 6. Real-replay comparison protocol (seeds 500–504)

No tracker is treated as ground truth. For each seed:

- Run all four trackers (v1, v2, baseline, proposed) on the SAME recorded
  raw observation stream (`r_i(t)`, `z_i(t)`, and the recorded intervention
  history `u(t)` — physical replay only, never re-simulated).
- Select a reference subject at a common, unambiguous time where the
  trackers agree (if one exists) for cross-tracker comparison; where no
  such time exists, this is stated explicitly ("trackers began tracking
  different subjects at t=X and t=Y respectively") rather than papered
  over by picking one tracker's subject as canonical.
- Report every disagreement between trackers alongside the raw
  observations at that step (positions + headings), so a reader can judge
  for themselves, per the mandate's "show every disagreement with raw
  observations" instruction.
- If feasible, obtain a small label-blind human event-annotation panel
  (annotators see only raw bird positions/headings, not any tracker's
  output, and may answer "ambiguous") for a sample of contested steps —
  explicitly disclosed as a soft corroboration signal, NOT unique ground
  truth for emergent identity (no such ground truth exists for a
  self-organizing collective by construction).

## 7. Fresh uncontrolled episodes (no new control yet)

Independent of the tracker comparison: run fresh uncontrolled episodes
with the physical model UNCHANGED (same primary regime as `RESULTS_6_11.md`
§1) and apply the proposed joint model to ask whether stable,
visually-recognizable multi-object lineages are found spontaneously,
including their emergence/termination rates — a phenomenology question,
answered with the new instrument, not a control-outcome question. No
actuation, no target heading, no intervention API calls in this part of
the protocol at all.

## 8. What this protocol does not authorize

Passing all dev-set scenarios does not authorize skipping the held-out run.
A strong held-out synthetic result does not authorize claiming the real
5-seed replay is "solved" (no ground truth exists there). Neither result
authorizes proceeding to Phase 4 (control integration) — that requires a
separate, later gate stated in `IDENTITY_MODEL.md` and the mandate's Phase
4 section, not this document.
