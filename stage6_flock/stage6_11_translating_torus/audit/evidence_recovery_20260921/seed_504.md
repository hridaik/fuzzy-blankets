- **Intervention-effect / causal evidence**: real, positive, measured

  repair failure; not proof of impossibility at a larger budget either.

## Lineage cleanliness vs. control outcome — explicitly not correlated in this sample

Seed 504 has the cleanest lineage tracking (0 switches, 0 duplicates, 0
zero-overlap events) of any seed and fails to turn; seeds 501–503 have
messy, duplicate-laden, multiply-overtaken lineages and are the three
originally-reported "successes." `LINEAGE_FORENSICS_6_11.md` §6 states this
explicitly and this audit's independent zero-overlap count (504: 0; 500: 2
genuine zero-overlap events pre-control, 0 during; 501: 1 during control;
502: 3; 503: 2) is consistent with, and does not overturn, that observation.
n=5 is too small to support any causal claim about lineage cleanliness
predicting control outcome, and none is made here.
