STAGE 6.11 SECTION M -- AUDIT OF THE MOVING MODEL, BEFORE CHANGES
2026-09-09

Subject: stage6_9_translating_collective/code/moving_flock.py, whose physics is
inherited entirely from flock_sim/model.py (base_R / neighbor_R / SLOT_OVERRIDES).

The brief requires checking whether EFFECTIVE versions of the three generic
moving-flock ingredients already exist, and forbids adding duplicates.

----------------------------------------------------------------
INGREDIENT 1 -- ALIGNMENT / HEADING MATCHING          ** PRESENT **
----------------------------------------------------------------
flock_sim/model.py:76 base_R()
        R = fc * (UV4 @ UV4.T)
        np.fill_diagonal(R, vm)          # vm = 4.0

The diagonal rewards adopting the same heading as the neighbour, at a strength
(vm = 4.0) four times the off-diagonal scale (fc = 1.0). This is velocity
matching. Nothing to add.

----------------------------------------------------------------
INGREDIENT 2 -- COLLISION AVOIDANCE / SEPARATION      ** PRESENT **
----------------------------------------------------------------
flock_sim/model.py:84 neighbor_R(), via SLOT_OVERRIDES
        R[o, s] = -ca                    # ca = 2.0

For each of the 8 neighbour directions, specific (neighbour-direction,
own-heading) pairs are penalised: heading into an occupied direction costs -ca.
moving_flock.py generalises this to continuous positions by assigning each
partner to the Moore SLOT whose octant it falls in (OCTANT_TO_SLOT), so a bird
approaching head-on still incurs the same penalty. Nothing to add.

----------------------------------------------------------------
INGREDIENT 3 -- CENTERING / COHESION                  ** PARTIAL **
----------------------------------------------------------------
What exists: the off-diagonal fc * dot(own heading, neighbour heading) term.
What does NOT exist: any term that depends on the neighbour's POSITION relative
to the bird, i.e. anything pulling a bird toward the local centre of mass.

Evidence that cohesion is heading-based only:
  * base_R is built from UV4 @ UV4.T -- an inner product of HEADING unit
    vectors. No position enters it.
  * moving_flock.compute_G weights every live partner identically; the only
    positional information used is the OCTANT (which slot), consumed by the
    collision term. There is no distance weighting and no centroid term.
  * Consequently a bird with all its neighbours to its north has no preference
    to move north; it only prefers to match their headings and to avoid heading
    into whichever octants they occupy.

WHY THIS WAS INVISIBLE UNTIL NOW
On the fixed lattice (Stages 6-6.8, 6.10) positions never change, so a
positional cohesion term would have no effect and its absence cannot be
detected. It becomes load-bearing only once birds translate.

LIKELY CONNECTION TO THE STAGE 6.9 FAILURE
Stage 6.9 obtained a trackable translating collective only at R = 1.6, where
realized in-degree was 21.2 against a Moore scale of 8 -- roughly 3x
over-connection -- and that same over-connection produced 77% policy saturation
and a 1-of-47 causal interface. With alignment as the ONLY force holding a group
together spatially, cohesion has to be bought with interaction range, and range
buys saturation. That is a plausible mechanism for "you can have a trackable
translating collective or an inferable interface, but not both", and it is a
hypothesis this stage can test, not a conclusion.

----------------------------------------------------------------
DECISION (recorded before any 6.11 experiment)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Ingredients 1 and 2 are present and are NOT duplicated.

Ingredient 3 is genuinely absent in its positional form. Section M permits
adding a genuinely absent ingredient once. It will be added as an explicitly
labelled, separately switchable term with the raw model retained as comparator,
in the same manner as the Section N degree normalisation -- i.e. as a MODELLING
ASSUMPTION, declared here in advance, not as a fix introduced after seeing a
result.

It is NOT added in order to make any trajectory work. The test of whether it
was the right call is Section O's uncontrolled (R, v) scan: if positional
cohesion holds a collective together at LOWER realized degree, then range and
decision gain have been successfully separated, which is the stated purpose of
Sections M-N. If it does not, that is reported and the term is kept off.

No parameter of it may be tuned on control success (Section V rule 6/7).
