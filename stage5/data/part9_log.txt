Peak transient leakage occurs at T_ramp=0.2 (max_t Lambda_3 = 3.786e-05), NOT at the fastest tested ramp. As T_ramp->0 the ramp becomes a near-instantaneous jump: the system has too little time to leave the endpoint-A covariance, at which {4,5,6} is ALREADY an exact (zero-leakage) size-3 separator (see Section 3), so leakage stays small. As T_ramp->infinity the quasi-static limit is recovered (Section 8, Lambda_3^*(z)=0 identically). Leakage is maximal at an INTERMEDIATE rate where the covariance has moved away from the T->0 degenerate-safe configuration but not yet relaxed onto the new instantaneous-equilibrium separator. Because the spec's requested primary set {0.25,...,8} starts just past this peak, the monotone-decrease finding above is correct for that set but should NOT be read as evidence of monotonicity over all rates -- the qualitative hypothesis (finite-rate remodeling can transiently destroy exact screening) holds, but its rate-dependence is non-monotone, not monotone.
