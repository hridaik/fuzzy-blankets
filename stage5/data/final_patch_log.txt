  - dynamics.py: z_rhs/moment_rhs/make_rhs/integrate gained an optional `tau_z=None`

INTERPRETATION -- four findings kept distinct
================================================================================
1. Baseline scientific result (UNCHANGED): at delta=0.01, the original Stage-5
   collective is robust and the pathwise-integrity constraint is normally inactive.
   Part 3 STRENGTHENS this finding with an explicit negative control: it remains
   inactive across a 64x range of interface-remodeling rates (tau_z from 0.0625 to
   4) and under unit-test thresholds as tight as 1e-5, task-only control alone.

2. Figure-6 correction: boundary-state membership changing over time is not identity
   loss; the correct object is the smallest boundary family continuing to meet the
   leakage tolerance, and that family now shows exactly {4,5}->{4,5,6}->{5,6} with
   no spurious supersets.

3. Organization result: D_org genuinely decreases (accumulated departure, A_org_ctrl)
   as lambda_org increases from 0 to 1 to 100, but at lambda_org=100 the optimizer's
   best-found solution notably fails to hit the task target -- so the tradeoff is real
   but the largest-lambda_org point should not be read as a feasible task solution.

4. Stress-test result: at the primary delta=0.01, the pathwise MB constraint machinery
   (formulation C) was exercised across a 64x range of tau_z and NEVER activated --
   this strengthens finding 1. Only at a deliberately extreme, non-physical unit-test
   tolerance (delta_unit=1e-6, ~100x tighter than the primary threshold and below the
   optimizer's own reliable numerical resolution at this horizon) did the constraint
   become active: formulation C's best-found solution measurably changed protocol
   (lower peak leakage, higher energy, different u_y/u_z shape) relative to task-only,
   though it could not be driven to full numerical satisfaction even at T=4 or with
   20 knots. This must NOT be read as evidence the baseline collective is fragile --
   it is a controller unit test at a threshold ~100x tighter than any biologically
   motivated value used elsewhere in Stage 5, and Figure 12 is captioned accordingly.
